Note for parents and teachers

If a child answers Job 2 correctly in their own words rather than repeating "breaks big things into small pieces" verbatim, that's a strong sign the idea of an enzyme actually landed, not just the word itself. Job 3 has no single "correct" wording, it's meant to start a conversation about why specialisation exists in nature.

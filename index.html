Who I Am

Cambridge Educator & Academic Leader

I am a CAIE Approved Examiner and Cambridge-experienced educator with over 8 years of experience delivering IGCSE and AS & A Level Global Perspectives & Research, and Biology at international and national curriculum schools across Tamil Nadu.

At The Indian Public School (Cambridge), Erode, I delivered IGCSE Global Perspectives and Lower Secondary Checkpoint curriculum, led internal coursework moderation and assessment standardisation in alignment with CAIE requirements, and trained my successor teacher — all documented through official school correspondence. Under my facilitation, students achieved a National Top Rank, multiple A* grades, and the highest number of Cambridge Gold Awards in the school's history.

Beyond the classroom, I am a published molecular biology researcher (IJLS, 2018), a Certified Peace Instructor (IWPG), a Geneva Peace Week participant, and an active science communicator with 14 published articles in Times NIE and The TIPSIAN Weekly on education, ESG, gender equity, geopolitics, and global citizenship.

I am also developing Rolling Through the Odds — a 6-module, SDG-aligned sustainability education course for corporates and sustainability platforms — with MoU partnership discussions currently underway. As a LinkedIn thought leader with 500+ connections across ESG, EdTech, geopolitics, and peace education, I regularly engage renowned directors and international professionals through content on sustainability, global perspectives, and education leadership.

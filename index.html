Who I Am

Cambridge Educator & Academic Leader

I am a Cambridge-certified educator and CAIE Approved Examiner with over 8 years of experience delivering IGCSE and AS & A Level Global Perspectives & Research, Biology, and interdisciplinary science at international schools across Tamil Nadu.

As Internal CAIE Moderator at The Indian Public School (Cambridge), I led curriculum design, assessment moderation, teacher development, and stakeholder engagement — achieving a National Top Rank, multiple A* results, and the highest number of Cambridge Gold Awards in the school's history.

Beyond the classroom, I am a published molecular biology researcher, a Certified Peace Instructor (IWPG), an active science communicator, and a mentor to early-career educators and professionals. I bring subject mastery, leadership discipline, and a genuine commitment to learning that transforms students.

I hold an M.Sc. in Biotechnology from Kongunadu Arts & Science College and have been publishing educational articles in Times NIE and The TIPSIAN Weekly since 2024.

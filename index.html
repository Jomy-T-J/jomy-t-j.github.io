Who I Am

Cambridge Educator & Academic Leader

Jomy T J CAIE Approved Examiner · 2026

In January 2025, one of my IGCSE Global Perspectives students was declared National Top Rank in India, with others securing A* results from the November 2024 examination series. My next cohort recorded the highest number of Cambridge Gold Awards in Lower Secondary Checkpoint Global Perspectives when results were declared in August 2025.

That is what eight years of practically diverse experience, with a focused commitment to Cambridge curriculum work, looks like in practice.

I am a CAIE Approved Examiner for AS and A Level Global Perspectives and Research (2026) and a Cambridge-experienced educator who has delivered IGCSE and AS & A Level Global Perspectives & Research, and Biology at international schools. I hold an M.Sc. in Biotechnology and have co-authored a peer-reviewed study on cardiovascular disease risk (IJLS, 2018).

Beyond teaching, I am developing Rolling Through the Odds — a 6-module, SDG-aligned sustainability course for corporates and sustainability platforms — and serve as a Career Mentor through the Global Mentorship Initiative internationally.
